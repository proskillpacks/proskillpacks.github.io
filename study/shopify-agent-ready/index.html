Check your own store

  1. Shipping and returns: one plain line on each product page (only what your policy says), real Shipping and Refund policy text, and shippingDetails / hasMerchantReturnPolicy in your product schema.
  2. Descriptions: 80+ words on your main products: materials, sizes, who it's for, care.
  3. Ratings: view the page source. If the star rating isn't in the HTML, turn on your reviews app's structured-data / rich-snippet setting.
  4. Identifiers: fill in variant barcodes (GTIN/UPC/EAN) and make sure brand and GTIN appear in the product schema.
  5. Access: check yourstore.com/robots.txt doesn't disallow AI search agents from /products/, and that bot-protection apps don't challenge them.

Method

  • Sample: 100 Shopify stores (99 measurable), chosen by hand. About 30 large brands, 40 mid-size and 30 small independents across the US, UK, EU, Australia/NZ and Canada, in 16 categories. It isn't a random sample of all Shopify stores, so read every figure as "in this sample".
  • Data: public storefront data only (robots.txt, product pages and JSON, structured data, policies). No logins, no orders, rate-limited requests.
  • Audit: run on 3 October 2026 from a UK IP address. Checks read the server-rendered HTML and structured data, which is what agents that don't run JavaScript see. We didn't test firewalls or bot protection. Each store was scored out of 100 across access, structured data, product content, policies and path to purchase. Checks that couldn't run are excluded from that store's score.
  • Publishing: aggregate results only. No store is named. Slices under 10 stores aren't shown.
